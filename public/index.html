PayHealth

Get your proof ready at every sale, so a PayPal dispute never finds you without it.

How this works

Start here: a sale comes in

PayHealth starts at the sale, not when a dispute arrives. It tells you which proof to keep while you still have it, and can send your tracking number to PayPal.

This makes a made-up paid order. Save some proof for it, then play the buyer and open a dispute. The reply is drafted from the proof you saved. Nothing is sent to PayPal.

Or jump straight to a dispute

Both cases are made up and labeled "Demo case". Nothing is sent to PayPal.

  • With proof: a buyer says a fabric order did not arrive. The seller saved tracking, a receipt and an email.
  • No proof saved: a buyer says sandals did not arrive. The seller saved nothing, so the reply cannot be approved.
